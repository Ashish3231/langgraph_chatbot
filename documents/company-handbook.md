# Acme Robotics - Employee Handbook (excerpt)

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside those hours you
are free to arrange your day as you like, as long as your work is done and your
team knows when to expect you.

## Annual leave
Every employee gets 28 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public
holidays. Leave is requested in Workday and needs your manager's approval.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the following year; anything above that
is lost on 31 December.

## Remote work
Acme is remote-first. You may work from anywhere in a country where we have a
legal entity. Working from another country for more than 30 days a year needs
approval from People Ops because of tax rules.

## Equipment
New joiners receive a laptop, a monitor and a 600 EUR budget for a desk, chair
and anything else they need. Replacement laptops are issued every three years.

## Expenses
Submit expenses within 60 days of the purchase. Meals with clients, travel and
conference tickets are covered. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
