# Acme Robotics - Support FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to acme.example.com/login and click "Forgot password". The reset link is
valid for one hour. If it has expired, request a new one - links cannot be
reused.

## The robot arm reports error E-42. What does it mean?
E-42 means the arm lost contact with its position sensor. Power the unit down,
reseat the sensor ribbon cable at connector J3, and power it back up. If the
error returns within an hour, the sensor board needs replacing.

## What is the warranty period?
Hardware is covered for 24 months from the date of delivery. The warranty covers
manufacturing defects only; damage from liquids, drops or unapproved
modifications is not covered.

## How long does a repair take?
Once we receive the unit, a standard repair takes 5 to 7 working days. Customers
on the Priority Support plan get a replacement unit shipped within 48 hours.

## Can I return an order?
Unopened orders can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened units
carry a 15% restocking fee.
